Reading theme

Pure black switches off pixels on AMOLED screens during long study sessions.

Screen layout

Desktop shows the saved topics on the left and the tables side by side. On a phone it shows the full page zoomed out, like Chrome's "Desktop site"; pinch to zoom in.

Keep your work safe

Your topics live in this phone's browser storage. Make a backup file every week and keep it in Google Drive or a pen drive. A backup restores everything on a new phone.

Install on the phone

Android (Chrome): open the app link, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
