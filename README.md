# Topic Lab

A study app for APPSC Group 1, Group 2 and civil services preparation. Add newspaper text, textbook photos, screenshots or links; Google Gemini (your own free key) turns them into study tables: subject, theme, summary, vocabulary with Telugu meanings, synonyms and antonyms, key terms, idioms, Telugu ↔ English translation, questions with answers, writing practice and report writing. Everything is saved on the device and works offline.

Open the app: https://manjulasivalakshmi143-dot.github.io/topic-lab/
